Three directions, same screens.

Each direction shows the desktop Home screen and the phone app's home screen with identical content, so only the look and feel changes. Pick one, or mix: for example A's warmth with B's density. These are static mockups for choosing a style, not final designs.

Direction A

Honey Glass

Warm, glowing and premium. Frosted glass layers over a honey-amber backdrop, soft light, generous rounding. Feels like a crafted product with a personality, dark by default.

FeelCozy, rich, a bit magical. Strong brand identity tied to "nectar".
StrengthsMost distinctive and memorable; Mica/blur shine here; great for screenshots and launch posts.
RisksGlass everywhere can hurt legibility and costs GPU; needs a strong light-mode variant.
Direction B

Graphite Pro

Calm, precise and fast. Near-black graphite surfaces, hairline borders, tight spacing, one amber accent used sparingly. Think Linear or Raycast: a power tool that gets out of the way.

FeelProfessional, focused, keyboard-first. Dense information without clutter.
StrengthsAges well, very legible, cheapest to render, scales to many features and screens.
RisksLess warmth and personality; could look like "another dev tool" unless motion carries the delight.
Direction C

Bloom Expressive

Bright, friendly and playful. Light cream surfaces, bold tonal color blocks, big rounded shapes and large type, in the spirit of Material 3 Expressive, so the PC app feels like a sibling of the phone app.

FeelJoyful and approachable; feels at home next to a modern Android phone.
StrengthsMost consistent with the Android app (M3 Expressive); friendliest for non-technical users.
RisksCan feel toy-like on a desktop; color blocks need restraint as features grow; needs a strong dark variant.
