# Known Limitations

NectarLink aims to be completely transparent about what works out of the box, what requires an optional setup step, and what is restricted by the Windows 11 or Android operating systems or device hardware.

## Summary of Platform and Hardware Limitations

| Area | Limitation | Why It Happens | Workaround or Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Calls: Two-way voice audio on PC** | You can see caller ID, answer, decline, dial, and (with `Elevated`) mute, hold, toggle speakerphone, and press keypad tones from your PC, but **call voice audio stays on the phone**. | Android routes cellular call audio (`VOICE_CALL`) in hardware DSP and blocks software capture. On Windows 11 22H2+, Microsoft's `PhoneLineTransportDevice` Bluetooth HFP API is locked to Microsoft's inbox system broker (`E_ACCESSDENIED` for third-party apps), and `AudioPlaybackConnection` is one-way A2DP media only (see [`docs/research/hfp.md`](../research/hfp.md)). | Use the **Speaker** button in NectarLink's call bar (with `Elevated`) or keep Bluetooth headphones paired to your phone via multipoint Bluetooth. |
| **Clipboard: Automatic background phone-to-PC sync** | At `Standard` and `Assist`, copying text on your phone does not send it to your PC automatically in the background. | Android 10 and newer block background apps from reading the system clipboard unless they run as the foreground app, the active keyboard (IME), or the `shell` user (`UID 2000`). | Enable the **`Elevated`** power level once (Wireless Debugging) for automatic background phone-to-PC clipboard sync, or tap the **Send clipboard** Quick Settings tile, share sheet target, or widget. |
| **Notifications: Sensitive text & OTPs on Android 15+** | At `Standard` and `Assist`, Android 15 and newer may redact notification text containing two-factor authentication codes from third-party notification listeners. | Android 15's Enhanced Confirmation Mode withholds sensitive notification content unless the listener holds the privileged `RECEIVE_SENSITIVE_NOTIFICATIONS` appop. | Enable the **`Elevated`** power level once. NectarLink's shell helper automatically grants `RECEIVE_SENSITIVE_NOTIFICATIONS` to NectarLink. |
| **Messages: Third-party chat app threads** | Unified Conversations from WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram, and RCS show messages that arrive while connected and let you reply via `RemoteInput`, but cannot load older historical chat databases or start a brand-new thread with a contact who has not messaged you. | Android sandboxes each third-party chat app's internal SQLite database and does not expose a public API for reading historical third-party messages or RCS databases. | Open the chat app directly on your PC in its own **`app window`** (`Elevated` on Android 11+) when you need to browse old chat history or start a new chat. |
| **Windows Integration: System Share sheet (`Win+Share`) & Widgets board (`Win+W`)** | File Explorer's right-click **Send to** menu, Explorer context menu, taskbar **Jump List**, drag-and-drop, and `--share` CLI work on all installs, but appearing in the Windows 11 `Win+Share` popup and `Win+W` Widgets board requires package identity. | Windows 11 requires `windows.shareTarget` and `com.microsoft.windows.widgets` COM extensions to belong to a package signed with a trusted code-signing certificate (or have Windows Developer Mode enabled). | Use File Explorer's right-click **Send to** menu, drag files to the `Shelf` (`Ctrl+Alt+S`), or turn on Windows Developer Mode and run setup again. If NectarLink still isn't in the Share sheet, `sparse-package.log` in its install folder says why. |
| **Mirroring: `FLAG_SECURE` windows** | Banking apps, password managers, and DRM-protected streaming video apps render black when mirrored at `Standard` or `Assist`. | Android's window compositor strips `FLAG_SECURE` layers from standard `MediaProjection` virtual displays to protect credentials and DRM video. | Normal apps and the Android home screen render normally as soon as you leave the protected window. |
| **Mirroring: `Elevated` `/dev/uhid` and `REMOTE_SUBMIX` on some OEM ROMs** | On some non-AOSP OEM Android ROMs, virtual physical keyboard/gamepad registration (`/dev/uhid`) or internal audio capture for apps that block playback capture (`REMOTE_SUBMIX`) may be denied to `shell` (`UID 2000`). | Custom OEM SELinux policies sometimes restrict `/dev/uhid` or `AUDIO_SOURCE_REMOTE_SUBMIX` to `system` (`UID 1000`) or `root` rather than `shell` (`UID 2000`). | NectarLink detects the permission denial automatically and falls back cleanly to standard `InputManager` key/touch injection and standard Android 10+ `AudioPlaybackCapture`. |
| **Webcam: 4K sensor support and 8-bit SDR color** | 4K (`2160p`) webcam streaming requires a physical phone camera sensor and hardware encoder that support 3840x2160 at 30 fps. Video is streamed in 8-bit SDR (`NV12` / `RGB32`), not 10-bit HDR. | Emulators and some front-facing phone cameras max out at 1080p. Windows Media Foundation virtual cameras in video conferencing apps expect standard 8-bit SDR formats (`NV12` / `RGB32`), so 10-bit HDR is omitted to avoid tone-mapping mismatches. | Use **1080p** or **720p** if your phone's selected camera lens does not support 4K capture. |
| **Wake-on-LAN & BLE Proximity Wake: Hardware dependencies** | Waking a powered-off PC over Wi-Fi rarely works, and Companion Device BLE proximity wake requires a Bluetooth LE radio on both the PC and the phone. | Most PC motherboards cut power to Wi-Fi cards during shutdown (`S5`), supporting Wake-on-LAN only over wired Ethernet or from low-power standby (`S0ix`/`S3`). Android emulators lack a Bluetooth LE controller. | Connect your desktop PC via Ethernet and enable **Wake on LAN** in Windows Device Manager and BIOS/UEFI for reliable Wake-on-LAN. |

## Detailed Notes on Key Platform Boundaries

### 1. Bluetooth Hands-Free Profile (HFP) Call Audio on Windows 11

Many users ask why NectarLink does not route two-way phone call audio through the PC's speakers and microphone over Bluetooth like Microsoft's pre-installed Phone Link app.

Microsoft's inbox `PhoneExperienceHost` (`YourPhone.exe`) uses an internal Windows system broker interface to `bthhfenum.sys` (`Windows.ApplicationModel.Calls.PhoneLineTransportDevice`). Starting in Windows 11 22H2, calling `PhoneLineTransportDevice::RequestAccessAsync()` or `Connect()` from any unpackaged or third-party application returns `E_ACCESSDENIED` because Windows checks for Microsoft's internal system identity and restricted capabilities (`phoneCallTransport` / `phoneCallOrigin`). Meanwhile, the public `Windows.Media.Audio.AudioPlaybackConnection` API only activates the one-way Bluetooth **A2DP Sink** profile for media playback, not a bidirectional eSCO voice channel for cellular calls. Implementing a third-party HFP Hands-Free unit on Windows 11 without Microsoft's restricted broker would require installing a custom WHQL-signed kernel-mode Bluetooth profile driver. Rather than bundling invasive kernel drivers or showing a control that fails silently, NectarLink provides full call signaling, caller ID, dialer, and `Elevated` in-call controls (mute, speakerphone, hold, keypad) while keeping voice audio on the phone.

### 2. Android Background Clipboard Access Without `Elevated`

Starting in Android 10, `ClipboardManager.getPrimaryClip()` returns `null` whenever the calling app does not have window focus. There is no runtime permission a user can grant in standard Android Settings to override this for a regular app. NectarLink solves this cleanly at both ends of the spectrum:

- If you stay at `Standard` or `Assist`, you can send your phone's clipboard with one tap from the **Send clipboard** Quick Settings tile (which briefly brings a transparent NectarLink activity to the front to read the clip and closes immediately), the Android share sheet, the home-screen widget, or the app.
- If you enable `Elevated` via Wireless Debugging, NectarLink's local shell helper (`UID 2000`) registers a system clipboard listener through Binder (`IClipboard`) so everything you copy on your phone arrives on your PC automatically in the background, while still honoring Android 13+'s `EXTRA_IS_SENSITIVE` flag to never copy passwords.

### 3. Windows Sparse Package Identity for `Win+Share` and `Win+W` Widgets

Unpackaged Win32/Qt applications on Windows 11 can register File Explorer context menus, `Send to` shortcuts, taskbar Jump Lists, global hotkeys, Notification Center toasts, Cloud Files (`cfapi`) sync roots, and Media Foundation virtual cameras (`MFCreateVirtualCamera`). All of those work out of the box in NectarLink.

However, Windows 11 restricts two shell extension points (`windows.shareTarget` in the system `Win+Share` dialog and `com.microsoft.windows.widgets` in the `Win+W` Widgets board) to applications that carry an MSIX or sparse Appx package identity signed by a certificate in the machine's Trusted People / Trusted Root store (or registered while Windows Developer Mode is turned on). NectarLink ships the complete implementation and `AppxManifest.xml` for both extensions, and documents this requirement honestly so users on standard unsigned builds know why `Send to` and the `Shelf` are the primary sharing entry points.
